Research Methodology

Overview

This section describes the methodology used for the development of a biomaterial patch containing Ganoderma lucidum extract as a potential topical antifungal formulation for dermatophytosis.

The research involved the preparation of two patch formulations with different polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) and gelatin concentrations. Glycerin was used as a plasticizer, while Ganoderma lucidum extract was incorporated into both formulations.

Formulation Development

Two formulations were prepared to investigate the use of different polymer compositions in the development of the biomaterial patch.

* Formulation 1: 10 g PVA, 5 g gelatin, 3 g glycerin, and 5 g Ganoderma lucidum extract.
* Formulation 2: 8 g PVA, 7 g gelatin, 3 g glycerin, and 5 g Ganoderma lucidum extract.

The final volume of each formulation was adjusted to 100 mL using distilled water.

Patch Preparation

The preparation process included separate dissolution of PVA and gelatin, polymer blending, glycerin incorporation, extract addition, casting, drying, patch removal, cutting, and packaging.

Detailed procedures for both formulations are provided in Patch Preparation.

Research Limitations

The physical appearance of the prepared patch alone does not establish its antifungal effectiveness, stability, or safety. These properties must be evaluated using appropriate experimental tests.

# Research Methodology

## Research Design
This project proposes the development of a biomaterial patch containing Lingzhi mushroom (Ganoderma lucidum) extract as a potential topical antifungal approach for zoonotic ringworm in humans.

## Research Stages
1. Literature review on Ganoderma lucidum and its antimicrobial properties.
2. Review of biomaterial patch formulations and topical drug delivery systems.
3. Planning of the patch formulation and preparation procedure.
4. Planning of physicochemical characterization and antifungal activity testing.
5. Evaluation of potential safety considerations and limitations.

## Planned Evaluations
- Physical properties of the patch
- Stability and suitability of the formulation
- In vitro antifungal activity
- Preliminary safety considerations

## Current Status
The methodology is at the planning stage. Experimental procedures, results, and conclusions have not yet been established.

## Notes
The final methodology will be refined based on verified scientific literature, laboratory feasibility, and applicable safety requirements.

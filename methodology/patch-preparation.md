# Patch Preparation Procedures

## 1. Materials

The materials used in the preparation of the biomaterial patch included polyvinyl alcohol (PVA), gelatin, glycerin, *Ganoderma lucidum* extract, and distilled water.

## 2. Formulation Composition

| Material | Formulation 1 | Formulation 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| PVA | 10 g | 8 g |
| Gelatin | 5 g | 7 g |
| Glycerin | 3 g | 3 g |
| *Ganoderma lucidum* extract | 5 g | 5 g |
| Distilled water | Added to a final volume of 100 mL | Added to a final volume of 100 mL |

## 3. Preparation of Formulation 1

1. **Material preparation:** All equipment and materials were prepared according to the composition of Formulation 1. The distilled water was divided into portions for dissolving PVA and gelatin.

2. **PVA dissolution:** A total of 10 g of PVA was dispersed in a portion of distilled water. The mixture was heated at approximately 80–90°C using a hot plate magnetic stirrer while stirring until a homogeneous solution was obtained.

3. **Gelatin dissolution:** A total of 5 g of gelatin was hydrated in a portion of distilled water for approximately 30 minutes. The gelatin was then heated at approximately 50–55°C while stirring until dissolved.

4. **Polymer solution conditioning:** The PVA solution was allowed to cool after dissolution. The gelatin solution was maintained at a sufficiently warm temperature to prevent premature gel formation.

5. **Polymer blending:** The gelatin solution was gradually added to the PVA solution while stirring until a homogeneous polymer mixture was obtained.

6. **Glycerin incorporation:** A total of 3 g of glycerin was added to the polymer mixture and stirred until evenly distributed.

7. **Cooling before extract addition:** The mixture was cooled to a suitable temperature before incorporating the extract.

8. **Extract incorporation:** A total of 5 g of *Ganoderma lucidum* extract was gradually added and mixed until evenly dispersed. Distilled water was added to obtain a final volume of 100 mL.

9. **Casting:** The mixture was poured into a clean silicone or glass mold and leveled to obtain a uniform layer.

10. **Drying:** The cast mixture was dried under the conditions used in the experiment. The drying temperature and duration should be documented using the actual experimental records.

11. **Demolding and cutting:** Once sufficiently dried, the patch sheet was removed from the mold and cut to the specified dimensions.

12. **Packaging:** The patches were placed in clean packaging, labeled as Formulation 1, and stored under the conditions used for subsequent testing.

## 4. Preparation of Formulation 2

1. **Material preparation:** All equipment and materials were prepared according to the composition of Formulation 2. The distilled water was divided into portions for dissolving PVA and gelatin.

2. **PVA dissolution:** A total of 8 g of PVA was dispersed in a portion of distilled water and heated at approximately 80–90°C while stirring until a homogeneous solution was obtained.

3. **Gelatin dissolution:** A total of 7 g of gelatin was hydrated in a portion of distilled water for approximately 30 minutes and then heated at approximately 50–55°C while stirring until dissolved.

4. **Polymer solution conditioning:** The PVA solution was allowed to cool, while the gelatin solution was maintained at a sufficiently warm temperature to prevent premature gel formation.

5. **Polymer blending:** The gelatin solution was gradually added to the PVA solution while stirring until homogeneous.

6. **Glycerin incorporation:** A total of 3 g of glycerin was added and mixed evenly into the polymer blend.

7. **Cooling before extract addition:** The mixture was cooled to a suitable temperature before adding the extract.

8. **Extract incorporation:** A total of 5 g of *Ganoderma lucidum* extract was gradually added and mixed until evenly dispersed. Distilled water was added to obtain a final volume of 100 mL.

9. **Casting:** The mixture was poured into a clean silicone or glass mold and leveled to obtain a uniform layer.

10. **Drying:** The cast mixture was dried under the conditions used in the experiment. The actual drying temperature and duration should be recorded from the experimental procedure.

11. **Demolding and cutting:** Once sufficiently dried, the patch sheet was removed from the mold and cut to the specified dimensions.

12. **Packaging:** The patches were packaged, labeled as Formulation 2, and stored under the conditions used for subsequent testing.

## 5. Experimental Notes

The procedures described above document the preparation of two patch formulations with different PVA-to-gelatin ratios.

The actual drying conditions, patch dimensions, and storage conditions should be reported according to the experimental records. Any deviations from the procedures should also be documented.

The preparation procedure alone does not establish antifungal effectiveness or product safety. These outcomes require appropriate experimental evaluation.
